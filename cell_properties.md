| No | Property                        | Brief description                                                                                    |
|----|---------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1  | Volume                          | Total 3D space occupied by the Cell or organelle.                                                    |
| 2  | Centroid                        | The geometric center of the organelle in (x, y, z) coordinates. Also the approximate center of mass. |
| 3  | X span                          | The extent of the organelle along the x-axis.                                                        |
| 4  | Y span                          | The extent of the organelle along the y-axis.                                                        |
| 5  | Z span                          | The extent of the organelle along the z-axis.                                                        |
| 6  | MIP Area                        | Maximum intensity projection area of the organelle in 2D in the XY plane.                            |
| 7  | 2D Aspect Ratio                 | Ratio of major to minor axis in the 2D projection.                                                   |
| 8  | Min feret                       | The shortest distance between two parallel tangents to the organelle.                                |
| 9  | Mean feret                      | The average of all possible Feret diameters.                                                         |
| 10 | Max feret                       | The longest distance between two parallel tangents to the organelle.                                 |
| 11 | Sphericity                      | Measure of how spherical the organelle is, compared to a perfect sphere.                             |
| 12 | Volume fraction                 | Ratio of the organelle’s volume to the total cell volume.                                            |
| 13 | Z-distribution                  | Distribution of organelle positions along the z-axis within the cell, wrt the cell centroid          |
| 14 | Radial distribution 2D          | Distribution of organelle positions from the cell center in the xy-plane.                            |
| 15 | Radial distribution 2D mean     | Mean of 2d radial distribution of the organelle in the xy-plane.                                     |
| 16 | Radial distribution 3D          | Distribution of organelle positions from the cell center in 3D space.                                |
| 17 | Mean 2D distance to wall        | Average shortest distance from the organelle to the cell membrane in 2D (XY plane).                  |
| 18 | Mean 3D distance to wall        | Average shortest distance from the organelle to the cell membrane in 3D.                             |
| 19 | Stdev 2D distance to wall       | Standard deviation of the shortest distances from the organelle to the cell membrane in 2D.          |
| 20 | Stdev 3D distance to wall       | Standard deviation of the shortest distances from the organelle to the cell membrane in 3D.          |
| 21 | Mean Top z-distance             | Mean distance of the organelle from the top of the cell along the z-axis.                            |
| 22 | Mean Bottom z-distance          | Mean distance of the organelle from the bottom of the cell along the z-axis.                         |
| 23 | Stdev Top z-distance            | Standard deviation of the organelle’s distance from the top of the cell.                             |
| 24 | Stdev Bottom z-distance         | Standard deviation of the organelle’s distance from the bottom of the cell.                          |
| 25 | Mean top surface z-distance     | Mean distance of the organelle from the top surface of the cell along the z-axis.                    |
| 26 | Mean bottom surface z-distance  | Mean distance of the organelle from the bottom surface of the cell along the z-axis.                 |
| 27 | Stdev top surface z-distance    | Standard deviation of the organelle’s distance from the top surface of the cell                      |
| 28 | Stdev bottom surface z-distance | Standard deviation of the organelle’s distance from the bottom surface of the cell                   |
|    |                                 |                                                                                                      |
